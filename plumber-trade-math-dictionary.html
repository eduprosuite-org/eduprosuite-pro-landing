Backflow Preventer

Mechanical device preventing contamination of potable water supply caused by backpressure or backsiphonage.

Building Drain

The lowest piping of a drainage system that receives discharge from inside stacks and conveys it to the building sewer beginning 30 inches outside the building wall.

Plumber Trade Math Blueprint

Drainage Fixture Unit (DFU)

Measure of the probable discharge into the drainage system by various types of plumbing fixtures used to size DWV piping.

Plumber Trade Math Blueprint

DWV (Drain, Waste, Vent)

Piping network that conveys waste away while venting sewer gases and balancing atmospheric pressure.

G
Plumber Trade Math Blueprint

Grade (Slope)

Fall per foot of horizontal piping. Pipes 2-inch or smaller require minimum 1/4 inch per foot (2%); 3 to 6-inch requires 1/8 inch per foot (1%).

Total Fall = Length (ft) * Slope (in/ft)
H
Plumber Trade Math Blueprint

Head Pressure (Hydraulic)

Pressure exerted by a vertical column of water. 1 vertical foot of water produces 0.434 PSI (or 1 PSI = 2.31 feet of head).

PSI = Head (ft) * 0.434
O
Plumber Trade Math Blueprint

Offset (45-Degree Constant)

Formula to calculate the travel of a 45-degree pipe offset using the constant 1.414.

Travel = Offset * 1.414
⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Deduct fitting take-offs from travel length before cutting pipe.
R
Plumber Trade Math Blueprint

Rolling Offset

An offset that changes piping direction in both horizontal (roll) and vertical (set) planes simultaneously.

True Offset = sqrt(Rise^2 + Roll^2); Travel = True Offset * 1.414
T
Plumber Trade Math Blueprint

Trap Arm Distance

Maximum allowable distance from fixture trap weir to vent fitting, determined by pipe diameter (e.g. 1-1/2 in pipe: max 6 feet under IPC).

Plumber Trade Math Blueprint

Trap Seal

Vertical distance between the crown weir and the top of the dip of a plumbing trap, standardly 2 to 4 inches.

W
Plumber Trade Math Blueprint

Water Supply Fixture Unit (WSFU)

Factor used to calculate demand on potable water distribution systems based on probability of concurrent fixture use.
