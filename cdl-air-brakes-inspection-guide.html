1. Why the Air Brake Test is an Automatic Fail

Unlike other parts of the pre-trip walkaround where missing an item results in point deductions, any error during the in-cab air brake check results in an immediate test failure under Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) CDL testing standards. You must perform the checks in sequential order, articulate what you are doing out loud, and state the exact PSI thresholds.

2. Steps 1 to 4: Static, Applied Leakage & Low Warning

Step 1: Chock Wheels & Build Pressure

Ensure wheels are chocked. Start engine and allow air pressure to build to governor cut-out (between $120$ and $140\text{ psi}$). Turn the engine OFF, then turn the electrical key ON (battery on, engine off).

Step 2: Static Leakage Test

Release the tractor protection valve (red knob) and parking brake valve (yellow knob) by pushing them IN. Wait for the initial pressure drop to stabilize. Time for 1 full minute using your watch. Air loss must not exceed $2\text{ psi}$ for a single vehicle or $3\text{ psi}$ for a combination vehicle.

Step 3: Applied Pressure Leakage Test (The Most Critical Step)

Press and hold the foot brake firmly. After the initial needle dip stabilizes, time for 1 full minute. Maximum allowed air loss:

  • Single vehicle: No more than $3\text{ psi}$ in 1 minute.
  • Combination vehicle (tractor + trailer): No more than $4\text{ psi}$ in 1 minute.
⚠️ Official Scoring Standard (Examiner Rule): You must hold the brake pedal steadily for a full 60 seconds. Releasing early or letting your foot slip causes an instant failure. Say aloud: "After the needle stabilized, I lost no more than 4 PSI in one minute."

Step 4: Low Air Warning Signal Check

Fan (pump) the brake pedal repeatedly to bleed air. The low air warning buzzer and dash light must activate between $55\text{ psi}$ and $75\text{ psi}$ (typically at or above $60\text{ psi}$).

3. Steps 5 to 7: Pop-Out, Build-Up & Cut-Out

Step 5: Spring Brake Pop-Out (Tractor Protection Valve)

Continue pumping the brake pedal. Between $20\text{ psi}$ and $45\text{ psi}$, the parking brake (yellow) and tractor protection valve (red) knobs must automatically POP OUT into emergency parking position.

Step 6: Air Pressure Build-Up Rate

Start the engine at normal operating idle (or fast idle ~1,000 RPM). Operating pressure must rise from $85\text{ psi}$ to $100\text{ psi}$ within 45 seconds in a dual air system.

Step 7: Air Compressor Governor Cut-Out & Cut-In

Allow pressure to build until the governor stops air delivery (audible 'sneeze' or dryer purge), typically between $120\text{ psi}$ and $140\text{ psi}$. Next, step on brakes to bleed pressure until the compressor kicks back in (cut-in pressure must not drop below $100\text{ psi}$).

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What order should the air brake test be performed?

Follow the standard CDL order: (1) Static test, (2) Applied pressure leak test, (3) Low air warning buzzer, (4) Emergency spring brake pop-out, (5) Build-up rate check, (6) Governor cut-out/cut-in.

\n

❓ What happens if my combination vehicle loses 5 PSI in 1 minute?

Your vehicle fails the safety inspection. A 5 PSI loss exceeds the 4 PSI FMCSA maximum threshold and is declared out-of-service until repaired.
