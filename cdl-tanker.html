Frequently Asked Questions

What is the definition of a tank vehicle under FMCSA rules?

Any commercial vehicle designed to transport liquid or gaseous material in a tank having an individual rated capacity of more than 119 gallons and aggregate of 1,000+ gallons.

Can I combine the Tanker and Hazmat endorsements?

Yes, taking both qualifies you for the lucrative X Endorsement (Tanker + Hazmat combination).
