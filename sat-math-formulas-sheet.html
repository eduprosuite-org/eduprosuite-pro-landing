1. What College Board Provides (Official Reference Sheet)

Clicking the reference icon in Bluebook displays:

  • Area of circle: $A = \pi r^2$, Circumference: $C = 2\pi r$
  • Area of rectangle: $A = \ell w$, Area of triangle: $A = \frac{1}{2} b h$
  • Pythagorean theorem: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$
  • Special right triangles: $30^\circ-60^\circ-90^\circ$ ($x, x\sqrt{3}, 2x$) and $45^\circ-45^\circ-90^\circ$ ($x, x, x\sqrt{2}$)
  • Volume formulas: Rectangular prism ($V = \ell w h$), Cylinder ($V = \pi r^2 h$), Sphere ($V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$), Cone ($V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$), Pyramid ($V = \frac{1}{3} \ell w h$)
  • Degrees in a circle: $360^\circ$, Radians in a circle: $2\pi$, Sum of angles in a triangle: $180^\circ$

2. Unprovided Algebra & Quadratic Formulas (Must Memorize)

Quadratic Forms & Vertex

$$y = ax^2 + bx + c \implies x_{\text{vertex}} = -\frac{b}{2a}$$
$$y = a(x - h)^2 + k \implies \text{Vertex at } (h, k)$$

Discriminant & Number of Solutions

For $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, evaluate $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$:

  • $\Delta > 0$: 2 distinct real solutions (2 $x$-intercepts)
  • $\Delta = 0$: 1 real solution (tangent to $x$-axis)
  • $\Delta < 0$: 0 real solutions (no $x$-intercepts)

Sum and Product of Roots (Vieta's Formulas)

$$x_1 + x_2 = -\frac{b}{a}, \qquad x_1 \cdot x_2 = \frac{c}{a}$$

This shortcut solves complex Module 2 polynomial questions in 5 seconds without factoring.

3. Circles & Advanced Coordinate Geometry

Standard Equation of a Circle

$$(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$$

Center is at $(h, k)$ and radius is $r = \sqrt{r^2}$. To convert general form $x^2 + y^2 + Dx + Ey + F = 0$, complete the square for $x$ and $y$.

Arc Length & Sector Area in Radians

$$\text{Arc Length } s = r \theta, \qquad \text{Sector Area } A = \frac{1}{2} r^2 \theta$$

where $\theta$ is the central angle measured in radians.
