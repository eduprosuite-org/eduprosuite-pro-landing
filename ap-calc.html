Essential AP Calculus BC & AB Direct Knowledge Base

What is the difference between AP Calculus AB and AP Calculus BC?

AP Calculus AB covers differential and integral calculus equivalent to one college semester (Calculus I). AP Calculus BC covers all AB material plus advanced techniques including integration by parts, Taylor and Maclaurin series, parametric equations, polar coordinates, and vector-valued functions (Calculus I and II).

What topics are exclusive to AP Calculus BC?

AP Calculus BC includes exclusive Unit 9 and Unit 10 topics: parametric equations, polar coordinates, vector-valued functions, integration by parts, partial fractions, improper integrals, and infinite sequences and series including Taylor polynomials.

How to score a 5 on AP Calculus BC?

To score a 5 on AP Calculus BC, master Unit 10 Taylor series, practice timed College Board free-response questions (FRQs) using the official scoring rubrics, memorize core derivative and integral formulas, and utilize graphing calculators proficiently for Section I Part B and Section II Part A.
