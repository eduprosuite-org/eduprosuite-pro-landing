1. Fundamental TVM Algebraic Equations

Single Lump-Sum Compounding & Discounting

$$FV_N = PV (1 + r)^N, \qquad PV = \frac{FV_N}{(1 + r)^N} = FV_N (1 + r)^{-N}$$

When compounding occurs $m$ times per year, state the periodic rate $r_s = r / m$ and total periods $N = n \times m$:

$$FV_N = PV \left(1 + \frac{r_s}{m}\right)^{m \times n}$$

Perpetuity & Growing Perpetuity Valuation

$$PV_{\text{Perpetuity}} = \frac{PMT}{r}, \qquad PV_{\text{Growing Perpetuity}} = \frac{PMT_1}{r - g}$$

2. TI BA II Plus Setup & The Golden Clear Routine

💡 Key Examination Standard (Exam Day Setup): Proctors reset calculators before the exam. Immediately execute:
1. Set Decimals to Floating / 9: Press [2nd] [FORMAT], type 9, press [ENTER].
2. Set Payments Per Year (P/Y) to 1: Press [2nd] [P/Y], type 1, press [ENTER]. (Default is often 12, which corrupts annual calculations).
3. Clear TVM Memory Before EVERY Calculation: Press [2nd] [CLR TVM].
Key Function Name Rules for Entry
N Total Number of Periods Enter integer or decimal representing periods ($years \times m$).
I/Y Interest Rate per Period Enter as percentage (5 for 5%, not 0.05).
PV Present Value Cash outflow must be entered as negative (use [+/-] key).
PMT Periodic Payment (Annuity) Consistent cash flow per period. Must have opposite sign of $FV$ if building an accumulation.
FV Future Value / Par Value Lump sum cash received or paid at horizon ($N$).

3. Ordinary Annuity vs. Annuity Due (END vs. BGN Mode)

An Ordinary Annuity pays at the end of each period (mortgages, bond coupons). An Annuity Due pays at the beginning of each period (leases, rent):

$$FV_{\text{Annuity Due}} = FV_{\text{Ordinary Annuity}} \times (1 + r)$$

To switch to Annuity Due mode: Press [2nd] [BGN], press [2nd] [SET], press [2nd] [QUIT]. BGN will display in the top-right corner. Remember to switch back to END immediately after!

4. Capital Budgeting: NPV & IRR Worksheet Keystrokes

For uneven cash flows, never use the TVM row. Use the [CF] worksheet:

  1. Press [CF], press [2nd] [CLR WORK].
  2. Enter initial outlay: CF0 = -100,000, press [ENTER], press [↓].
  3. Enter year 1 cash flow: C01 = 30,000, press [ENTER], press [↓], F01 = 1, press [↓].
  4. Enter subsequent cash flows (C02, C03, etc.).
  5. Calculate NPV: Press [NPV], enter discount rate into I = 10, press [ENTER], press [↓], press [CPT].
  6. Calculate IRR: Press [IRR], press [CPT].

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Why is my calculated TVM answer coming out with an Error 5?

Error 5 indicates conflicting cash flow signs. In financial arithmetic, an investment requires a cash outflow (negative PV) to produce a cash inflow (positive FV or PMT). If both are positive, the calculator cannot solve for interest rate I/Y.

❓ What is Effective Annual Rate (EAR) formula?

EAR represents the annual rate of interest actually being earned or paid after compounding: $\text{EAR} = (1 + r/m)^m - 1$. Use the BA II Plus ICONV worksheet (`[2nd] [ICONV]`) to convert NOM to EFF instantly.
