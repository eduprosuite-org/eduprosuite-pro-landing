People Also Ask (PAA) — EPA 608 Certification FAQ

Does an EPA Section 608 certification ever expire?

No. Once earned, an EPA 608 technician certification does not expire and remains valid for life unless revoked by the Environmental Protection Agency for regulatory violations.

What gas should NEVER be used for leak checking HVAC systems?

Never use pure oxygen or compressed acetylene to pressurize an HVAC system for leak checking. Oxygen mixed with compressor oil or refrigerant forms a violent explosive mixture.

What are the standard color codes for a dedicated refrigerant recovery cylinder?

DOT and EPA regulations mandate that approved recovery cylinders must have a gray body and a yellow top/shoulder.
