1. Foundations & Quantitative Volatility Forecasting

Integrates capital allocation performance measures with dynamic volatility updating models.

$$\text{RAROC} = \frac{\text{Earnings} - \text{EL}}{\text{Economic Capital}}, \quad \text{EWMA: } \sigma_n^2 = \lambda \sigma_{n-1}^2 + (1-\lambda) u_{n-1}^2, \quad \text{GARCH(1,1): } \sigma_n^2 = \omega + \alpha u_{n-1}^2 + \beta \sigma_{n-1}^2$$
⚠️ Common Candidate Trap: Failure of mean-reversion when $\alpha + \beta \ge 1$ in GARCH.
💡 Why This Step? (Curriculum Rationale): Risk models must adapt dynamically to volatility regimes while anchoring to long-run capital stability.

2. Financial Markets & Option Greeks

Pricing derivatives contracts and decomposing non-linear exposures into actionable Greek hedge ratios.

$$\text{Swap: } V = B_{\text{fix}} - B_{\text{flt}}, \quad \Delta = N(d_1), \quad \Gamma = \frac{N'(d_1)}{S \sigma \sqrt{T}}, \quad \mathcal{V} = S \sqrt{T} N'(d_1)$$
⚠️ Common Candidate Trap: Ignoring Gamma curvature when hedging large directional market swings.
💡 Why This Step? (Curriculum Rationale): Linear Delta hedging alone leaves substantial unhedged tail exposure.

3. Value at Risk & Coherent Expected Shortfall

Standardizes portfolio tail losses under parametric, historical, and coherent risk paradigms.

$$\text{VaR} = z_\alpha \cdot \sigma \cdot \sqrt{T} \cdot P, \quad \text{ES} = E[L \mid L > \text{VaR}], \quad \text{Square Root Rule: } \text{VaR}_T = \text{VaR}_1 \times \sqrt{T}$$
⚠️ Common Candidate Trap: Assuming returns are independent across time when applying the square root rule.
💡 Why This Step? (Curriculum Rationale): Provides a single, standardized dollar metric for corporate risk governance.
