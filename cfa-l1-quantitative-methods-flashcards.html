1. Time Value of Money (TVM) & Effective Annual Rate (EAR)

Where $r_{\text{stated}}$ is the nominal annual rate and $m$ is the compounding frequency per year. As $m \to \infty$, discrete compounding converges to continuous exponential compounding $e^r - 1$.

$$\text{EAR} = \left(1 + \frac{r_{\text{stated}}}{m}\right)^m - 1, \quad \text{Continuous Compounding: } \text{EAR} = e^{r_{\text{stated}}} - 1, \quad PV_{\text{perpetuity}} = \frac{PMT}{r}$$
⚠️ Common Candidate Trap: Compounding frequency misalignment: Forgetting to adjust your financial calculator payment periods (P/Y = 1, C/Y = 1 vs. N and I/Y conversions) causes catastrophic valuation errors.
💡 Why This Step? (Curriculum Rationale): Compounding interest generates exponential returns because interest earned in prior periods itself generates subsequent interest.

2. Bayes' Formula & Conditional Expectation

Updates the prior probability $P(A)$ of an economic state or earnings beat given newly released financial signals $B$ to determine the updated posterior probability.

$$P(\text{Event } A \mid \text{Information } B) = \frac{P(B \mid A)}{P(B)} \times P(A) = \frac{P(B \mid A) P(A)}{P(B \mid A) P(A) + P(B \mid A^c) P(A^c)}$$
⚠️ Common Candidate Trap: Confusing $P(B|A)$ with $P(A|B)$: The probability of positive news given high earnings is NOT identical to the probability of high earnings given positive news!
💡 Why This Step? (Curriculum Rationale): Bayesian inference rigorously balances prior base-rate probabilities against the statistical diagnostic power of newly acquired evidence.

3. Hypothesis Testing & Type I vs. Type II Errors

Type I error ($lpha$) is rejecting a true null hypothesis (false alarm). Type II error ($eta$) is failing to reject a false null hypothesis. Significance level is $lpha$.

$$\text{Test Stat} = \frac{\text{Sample Stat} - \text{Hypothesized Value}}{\text{Standard Error}}, \quad \text{Power} = 1 - P(\text{Type II Error}) = 1 - \beta$$
⚠️ Common Candidate Trap: Saying 'accept the null': Analysts NEVER accept $H_0$; we merely state that evidence is insufficient to reject $H_0$ at significance level $lpha$.
💡 Why This Step? (Curriculum Rationale): Statistical tests quantify evidence against a baseline null assertion under the presumption of innocence.
