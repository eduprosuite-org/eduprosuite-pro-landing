  • Process: To initiate a return, refund, or exchange, simply email our support team at support@eduprosuite.pro with your order details. Refunds are processed within 2-3 business days to your original payment method.

6. Shipping & Delivery Policy

We provide clear, transparent shipping and delivery timelines for all educational products and study guides:

  • Shipping Destinations: We ship to the United States, India, and internationally.
  • Shipping Costs: Free Standard Shipping ($0.00 / ₹0) on all student editions, exam prep suites, and workbook orders.
  • Handling & Processing Time: Orders are processed and dispatched within 0 to 1 business day (Monday – Friday).
  • Estimated Delivery Time: Standard delivery arrives within 2 to 5 business days (Monday – Friday).
  • Tracking: Real-time tracking numbers are automatically emailed upon dispatch.
