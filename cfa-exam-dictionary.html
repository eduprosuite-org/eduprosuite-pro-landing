CAPM (Capital Asset Pricing Model)

Calculates expected return on an asset based on systematic risk (Beta).

E(R) = R_f + Beta * [E(R_m) - R_f]

DuPont Analysis (3-Step & 5-Step)

Decomposes Return on Equity (ROE) into Net Profit Margin, Asset Turnover, and Financial Leverage.

ROE = (NI / Sales) * (Sales / Assets) * (Assets / Equity)
