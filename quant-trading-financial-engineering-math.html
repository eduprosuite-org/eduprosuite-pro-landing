Itô's Lemma & Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM)

$$dS_t = \mu S_t dt + \sigma S_t dW_t \implies S_t = S_0 \exp\left(\left(\mu - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2\right)t + \sigma W_t\right), \quad (dW_t)^2 = dt$$

📘 Mathematical & Clinical Mechanics

Because Brownian motion has non-zero quadratic variation, the second-order Taylor term $ rac{1}{2}\sigma^2 S^2 rac{\partial^2 f}{\partial S^2} dt$ survives in continuous-time stochastic calculus.

⚠️

Candidate & Practitioner Trap Alert

The Jensen inequality drift drag: The median growth rate is $\mu - rac{1}{2}\sigma^2$, NOT $\mu$. Ignoring volatility drag $ rac{1}{2}\sigma^2$ overestimates long-term asset compounding.

The Kelly Criterion for Optimal Position Sizing

$$f^* = \frac{p(b+1) - 1}{b} = \frac{p}{a} - \frac{q}{b}, \quad \text{Continuous: } f^* = \frac{\mu - r}{\sigma^2}$$

📘 Mathematical & Clinical Mechanics

Maximizes expected logarithmic utility $\mathbb{E}[\ln(W)]$, guaranteeing asymptotic capital growth faster than any other position-sizing strategy.

⚠️

Candidate & Practitioner Trap Alert

Full Kelly ruin volatility: Full Kelly trading subjects portfolios to massive $50\%+$ drawdowns. Institutional quantitative hedge funds strictly apply Half-Kelly ($0.5 f^*$) or Fractional Kelly.
