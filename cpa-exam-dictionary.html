Audit Assertions (AU-C 315)

Management representations embodied in financial statements: Completeness, Existence/Occurrence, Accuracy/Valuation, Rights and Obligations, Cutoff, and Presentation/Disclosure.

⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Vouching (ledger to source document) tests Existence; Tracing (source document to ledger) tests Completeness.
D
CPA® Review Blueprint

Deferred Tax Asset / Liability (ASC 740)

Differences between financial statement carrying amounts and tax bases of assets/liabilities. Temporary differences result in future taxable (liability) or deductible (asset) amounts.

G
CPA® Review Blueprint

GAAP vs IFRS Divergence

Key accounting standards divergence: IFRS prohibits LIFO inventory costing; IFRS permits revaluation of PP&E; GAAP requires direct development cost expensing while IFRS capitalizes development when criteria met.

M
CPA® Review Blueprint

MACRS Depreciation

Modified Accelerated Cost Recovery System for US tax depreciation. Half-year convention assumes property placed in service at mid-point of tax year regardless of actual acquisition date.

R
CPA® Review Blueprint

Revenue Recognition (ASC 606)

5-step framework: 1. Identify contract; 2. Identify performance obligations; 3. Determine transaction price; 4. Allocate transaction price; 5. Recognize revenue when obligations satisfied.
