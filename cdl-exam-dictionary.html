CDL Exam Blueprint

Air Compressor Governor

Controls when the air compressor pumps air into storage tanks. Cut-out pressure must be between 120 and 140 PSI; cut-in pressure is typically 100 PSI.

B
CDL Exam Blueprint

Brake Lag Distance

The distance vehicle travels during the delay between pressing the brake pedal and air flowing through lines to apply brake shoes (approximately 32 feet at 55 mph).

H
CDL Exam Blueprint

Hazmat Placards

Diamond-shaped signs (10.8 inches square) affixed to all four sides of commercial vehicles transporting regulated quantities of hazardous materials.

L
CDL Exam Blueprint

Low Air Pressure Warning

Safety buzzer/light that must activate at or above 55–60 PSI before tank pressure drops below safe operating thresholds.

S
CDL Exam Blueprint

Slack Adjuster Pushrod Stroke Limits

Maximum legal brake stroke distance. For standard Type 30 clamp-type chambers, pushrod stroke must not exceed 2 inches.
