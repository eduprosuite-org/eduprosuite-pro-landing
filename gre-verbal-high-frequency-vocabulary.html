1. Sentence Equivalence: The Twin Synonym Strategy

You must select TWO answer choices that complete the sentence with IDENTICAL meaning. Finding a word that fits the blank is useless if it has no twin partner among the choices!

$$\text{Step 1: Inspect the 6 choices and find matching synonym pairs (usually 2 distinct pairs + 2 decoys).} \\ \text{Step 2: Read the sentence blank and determine charge (+ or -). Eliminate mismatched pairs.}$$
⚠️ Common Candidate Trap: Picking a word that works in isolation without a synonym twin: Both chosen words MUST produce sentences that are virtually identical in nuance.
💡 Why This Step? (Curriculum Rationale): Tests lexical precision and sensitivity to subtle shades of semantic meaning.

2. Text Completion: Connotation & Structural Pivots

Before reading the answer choices, determine the positive ($+$) or negative ($-$) algebraic sign of the blank based on structural transition words.

$$\text{Positive / Agree Pivots: and, furthermore, because, similarly, therefore} \\ \text{Negative / Contrast Pivots: although, despite, whereas, however, paradoxically}$$
⚠️ Common Candidate Trap: Projecting outside knowledge into the passage: Gre sentences are self-contained logical universes; the clue is always embedded directly in the text.
💡 Why This Step? (Curriculum Rationale): Prevents subjective bias by enforcing strict textual evidence constraints.

3. Etymological Roots & Mnemonic Trees

Memorizing 50 foundational Greek and Latin roots unlocks the meaning of over 500 advanced academic words on test day.

$$\text{Root: 'MAL' (Bad) } \implies \text{maladroit (clumsy), malfeasance (wrongdoing), malevolent (evil)} \\ \text{Root: 'BELL' (War) } \implies \text{belligerent (aggressive), bellicose (warlike), rebellion}$$
⚠️ Common Candidate Trap: False cognates: 'Restive' does NOT mean restful; it means restless, unruly, and stubborn! (From Latin 'restare' - to pull back stubbornly).
💡 Why This Step? (Curriculum Rationale): Root morphology provides durable neural anchors for fast active recall.
