Ampacity (NEC Article 310)

Maximum current in amperes that a conductor can carry continuously under the conditions of use without exceeding its temperature rating.

⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Apply temperature correction and conductor bundling adjustment factors (Table 310.15).

Electrician NEC® Blueprint

Box Sizing (NEC Article 314.16)

Calculations determining maximum conductors allowed in outlet and junction boxes based on cubic inch volume allowances (e.g. 14 AWG = 2.0 cu in, 12 AWG = 2.25 cu in).

C
Electrician NEC® Blueprint

Conduit Bending Multiplier

Mathematical constant used to calculate distance between marks when bending offsets in electrical conduit.

30 deg = 2.0; 45 deg = 1.414; 22.5 deg = 2.6; 10 deg = 6.0
G
Electrician NEC® Blueprint

Grounding vs Bonding (NEC Article 250)

Grounding connects the system electrical circuit to the earth (earth ground). Bonding electrically connects conductive metal parts together to establish an effective fault-current path.

M
Electrician NEC® Blueprint

Motor Branch Circuit Protection (Article 430)

Sizing rules for motor branch circuit short-circuit and ground-fault protective devices (e.g. Inverse Time Breaker standard sizing up to 250% of Motor FLA).

Electrician NEC® Blueprint

Ohm's Law & Power Formulas

Fundamental electrical equations relating voltage (V), current (I), resistance (R), and power (P).

V = I * R; P = V * I; 3-Phase Power = sqrt(3) * V * I * PF
V
Electrician NEC® Blueprint

Voltage Drop Formula

Calculates voltage loss over long conductor runs to keep drop within NEC 3% recommendation for branch circuits.

VD = (2 * K * I * L) / CM (Single Phase)
