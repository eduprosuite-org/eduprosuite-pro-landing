Action Potential

Rapid electrical signal generated across neuronal membranes: resting potential (-70 mV), depolarization (Na+ influx), repolarization (K+ efflux), and hyperpolarization.

Henderson-Hasselbalch Equation

Calculates pH of buffer solutions using pKa and ratio of conjugate base to weak acid.

pH = pKa + log([A-] / [HA])

Michaelis-Menten Kinetics

Model of enzyme kinetics describing reaction rate v as a function of substrate concentration [S].

v = (Vmax * [S]) / (Km + [S])
⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Km is the substrate concentration at 1/2 Vmax; low Km signifies higher enzyme-substrate affinity.

Operant vs Classical Conditioning

Classical conditioning pairs involuntary responses with conditioned stimuli (Pavlov); operant conditioning modifies voluntary behaviors via positive/negative reinforcement or punishment (Skinner).
