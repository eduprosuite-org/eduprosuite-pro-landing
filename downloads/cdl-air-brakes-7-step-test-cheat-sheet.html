Core Governing Formula / Rule:
Single Vehicle Static Leak <= 2 PSI/min | Applied Leak <= 3 PSI/min. Combination Vehicle Static Leak <= 3 PSI/min | Applied Leak <= 4 PSI/min. Low Air Warning triggers at >= 60 PSI. Spring Brakes pop out between 20-45 PSI.

Step-by-Step Verified Application Example:

Engine Off, Wheels Chocked, Air at Governor Cutout (120-140 PSI).
1. Turn key to ON, release parking brake: observe static gauge for 1 min.
2. Fully depress foot brake: hold 1 min (must not drop > 3 PSI single / 4 PSI combo).
3. Fan brake pedal: warning buzzer/light MUST activate at or before 60 PSI.
4. Continue fanning: emergency tractor protection valves must pop out between 20 and 45 PSI.
