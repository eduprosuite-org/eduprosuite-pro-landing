2. Rolling Offset 2-Step Calculation Procedure
Step 1: Calculate True (Compound) Offset

A rolling offset changes both elevation (Rise) and horizontal position (Roll):

True Offset = √(Rise² + Roll²)
Example: Rise = 9", Roll = 12" → True Offset = √(81 + 144) = √225 = 15.00"
Step 2: Calculate Travel Length

Multiply True Offset by the angle fitting multiplier (for 45° fittings):

Travel (Center-to-Center) = True Offset × 1.414
Example: Travel = 15" × 1.414 = 21.21" (approx 21-3/16")
Step 3: Cut Length (End-to-End) Deduction
Cut Pipe Length = Travel - (Fitting Takeoff × 2)
Deduct the manufacturer center-to-face allowance for both fittings.
3. Hydrostatic Pressure & Volume Core Formulas
Metric / Concept Standard Formula Constant to Memorize
Static Head Pressure Pressure (PSI) = Head (Feet) × 0.434 1 Vertical Foot of Water = 0.434 PSI
Head from PSI Head (Feet) = Pressure (PSI) × 2.31 1 PSI = 2.31 Feet of Water Column
Water Weight in Pipe Gallons × 8.34 lbs = Total Weight (lbs) 1 Gallon of Water = 8.34 lbs (approx 8.33)
Volume of Cylindrical Pipe Gallons = D² (inches) × Length (feet) × 0.0408 Quick field volume multiplier
