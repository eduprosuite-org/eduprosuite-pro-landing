2. Rolling Offset 2-Step Sequence
Step 1 (True Offset): True Offset = √(Rise² + Roll²)
Step 2 (Travel Piece): Travel = True Offset × 1.414 (for 45° fittings)
Step 3 (Cut Length): Cut Length = Travel - (Fitting Takeoff × 2)
