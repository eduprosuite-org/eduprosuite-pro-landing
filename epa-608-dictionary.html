Azeotropic Mixture

A refrigerant blend made of two or more components that act as a single substance; it evaporates and condenses at a constant temperature without fractionating (e.g. R-502).

EPA 608 Blueprint

ASHRAE Safety Groups

Classification of refrigerant toxicity (A = lower, B = higher) and flammability (1 = no flame, 2L = lower flammability, 2 = flammability, 3 = higher flammability).

C
EPA 608 Blueprint

Clean Air Act Section 608

US federal law prohibiting the venting of ozone-depleting substances (ODS) and their substitutes into the atmosphere while servicing or disposing of equipment.

EPA 608 Blueprint

Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)

Refrigerants containing chlorine, fluorine, and carbon with the highest Ozone Depletion Potential (ODP), banned from production in 1995 (e.g. R-11, R-12).

D
EPA 608 Blueprint

De-Minimis Release

The minor, unavoidable release of refrigerant that occurs during normal connection/disconnection of hoses while adhering to proper recovery techniques.

E
EPA 608 Blueprint

Evacuation Levels (Vacuum)

Required vacuum levels in inches of Mercury (in. Hg) or microns required before opening appliances for repair.

Core target: 500 microns deep vacuum
⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Never use a system compressor to pull a deep vacuum; motor windings will burn out.
H
EPA 608 Blueprint

Hydrochlorofluorocarbons (HCFCs)

Refrigerants containing hydrogen, chlorine, fluorine, and carbon (e.g. R-22) phased out by 2020 with lower ODP than CFCs.

EPA 608 Blueprint

Hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs)

Refrigerants containing no chlorine (zero ODP) but high Global Warming Potential (GWP), subject to phase-down under the AIM Act (e.g. R-410A, R-134a).

L
EPA 608 Blueprint

Leak Repair Trigger Rates

Annual leak rate thresholds for appliances with 50+ lbs of refrigerant: Commercial Refrigeration: 20%; Industrial Process: 30%; Comfort Cooling: 10%.

⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Commercial is 20%, Industrial is 30%, Comfort is 10% (under updated Section 608 regulations).
M
EPA 608 Blueprint

Montreal Protocol

1987 international treaty phasing out production and consumption of ozone-depleting substances globally.

P
EPA 608 Blueprint

Passive Recovery (System-Dependent)

Capturing refrigerant with the assistance of components within the appliance (e.g. compressor) into a non-pressurized container, permitted only for Type I small appliances (<= 5 lbs).

R
EPA 608 Blueprint

Recovery Cylinder Regulations

DOT-approved recovery cylinders must have yellow tops, gray bodies, and be hydrostatically tested every 5 years with a maximum fill limit of 80% liquid capacity.

T
EPA 608 Blueprint

Type I Appliance

Hermetically sealed small appliance manufactured, charged, and hermetically sealed in a factory with 5 lbs or less of refrigerant.

EPA 608 Blueprint

Type II Appliance

High-pressure or very high-pressure appliances containing more than 5 lbs of refrigerant (split systems, rooftop packages, chillers).

EPA 608 Blueprint

Type III Appliance

Low-pressure appliances utilizing refrigerants with boiling points above 50 deg F at atmospheric pressure (e.g. CFC-11, HCFC-123).
