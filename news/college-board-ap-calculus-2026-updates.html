📅 Verified for 2026–2027 Examination Blueprint (Latest CED Edition)
By: Dr. Eleanor Vance, Lead Mathematics Assessment Fellow • Published: 2026-10-08

The College Board has confirmed the official schedule and policy updates for the 2026 Advanced Placement examinations. For AP Calculus AB and BC, the national test administration will take place during the second week of the May testing window, featuring standard morning testing sessions across all global regional time zones.

Key updates include a reaffirmed policy on graphing calculators for Section I Part B (Multiple Choice) and Section II Part A (Free Response). Students are strictly required to utilize approved graphing models equipped with built-in numerical derivative and definite integral computation capabilities. CAS (Computer Algebra System) functionality remains subject to standard proctoring verification.

Pedagogical coordinators note that Unit 10 (Infinite Sequences and Series) in AP Calculus BC retains its benchmark 17-18% weight on the exam. Educators and candidates are advised to prioritize Taylor polynomials, radius of convergence, and alternating series error bounds during spring preparation phases.
