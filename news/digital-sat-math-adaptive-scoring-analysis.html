Digital SAT 2026 Scoring: Module 2 Percentile Breakdown

By: Marcus Sterling, Senior Psychometric Specialist • Published: 2026-10-07

With the nationwide deployment of the Digital SAT via Bluebook, psychometric analysis reveals significant insights into how multistage adaptive routing influences composite score trajectories. Scoring data underscores that performance in Module 1 serves as the decisive gating mechanism.

Students achieving an accuracy rate above 70% on the baseline Module 1 are routed to the upper-tier Module 2, enabling a score ceiling of 800. Routing to the standard Module 2 caps potential math performance at approximately 590 to 620 regardless of perfect accuracy in the second module.

Educators recommend focused pacing and strategic error minimization during the initial 22 questions of Module 1, especially in advanced algebra and non-linear function modeling.
