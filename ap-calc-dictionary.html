Antiderivative

A function F(x) whose derivative equals f(x), denoted by integral f(x) dx = F(x) + C.

d/dx [F(x) + C] = f(x)
⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Never forget the arbitrary constant +C on indefinite integrals.
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Asymptote (Vertical & Horizontal)

A line that the graph of a function approaches arbitrarily closely. Horizontal: lim(x->inf) f(x) = L. Vertical: lim(x->c) f(x) = +-inf.

⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Check both positive and negative infinity for rational and exponential functions.
C
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Chain Rule

Formula for computing the derivative of the composite of two functions f(g(x)).

d/dx [f(g(x))] = f'(g(x)) * g'(x)
⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Always multiply by the derivative of the inside function g'(x).
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Concavity & Inflection Point

A graph is concave up where f''(x) > 0 and concave down where f''(x) < 0. An inflection point requires f''(x) to change sign.

⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: f''(x) = 0 is a candidate, NOT a guarantee of an inflection point.
D
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Definite Integral

The net signed area between the graph of f(x) and the x-axis from x = a to x = b.

int[a to b] f(x) dx = F(b) - F(a)
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Differentiability

A function is differentiable at x = c if f'(c) exists. Differentiability strictly implies continuity, but continuity does NOT imply differentiability (e.g. sharp corners |x|).

⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Do not assume continuity implies differentiability.
F
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (FTC Part 1)

Relates differentiation and integration for accumulation functions.

d/dx [int[a to x] f(t) dt] = f(x)
⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: If the upper bound is g(x), apply the Chain Rule: f(g(x)) * g'(x).
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (FTC Part 2)

Evaluates definite integrals via antiderivatives.

int[a to b] f(x) dx = F(b) - F(a)

AP® Calculus Blueprint

Implicit Differentiation

Technique used to differentiate equations where y is not expressed explicitly as a function of x.

d/dx [y^2] = 2y * (dy/dx)
⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Remember to attach (dy/dx) every time you differentiate a y term with respect to x.
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Intermediate Value Theorem (IVT)

If f is continuous on [a, b], then f takes on every value between f(a) and f(b).

⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Must state that f is continuous on the closed interval [a, b] in FRQs.
L
AP® Calculus Blueprint

L'Hôpital's Rule

Evaluates indeterminate limits of the forms 0/0 or +-inf/+-inf by differentiating numerator and denominator separately.

lim f(x)/g(x) = lim f'(x)/g'(x)
⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Never use the quotient rule here; verify the indeterminate form first.
M
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Mean Value Theorem (MVT)

If f is continuous on [a, b] and differentiable on (a, b), there exists c in (a, b) where instantaneous rate equals average rate.

f'(c) = (f(b) - f(a)) / (b - a)
⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Verify both continuity on [a,b] AND differentiability on (a,b).
P
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Product Rule

Derivative of the product of two differentiable functions.

d/dx [u * v] = u'v + uv'
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Power Rule

Formula for differentiating powers of x.

d/dx [x^n] = n * x^(n-1)
Q
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Quotient Rule

Derivative of the quotient of two differentiable functions.

d/dx [u / v] = (u'v - uv') / v^2
⚠️ Exam Trap Alert: Low d-high minus high d-low over the square of what's below.
R
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Riemann Sum

Approximation of the area under a curve using rectangles (Left, Right, Midpoint) or trapezoids.

Area ~= sum[i=1 to n] f(x_i) * delta_x
T
AP® Calculus Blueprint

Taylor & Maclaurin Series (BC Only)

Polynomial representation of an infinitely differentiable function centered at x = a (Maclaurin centered at 0).

f(x) = sum [f^(n)(a) / n!] * (x - a)^n
